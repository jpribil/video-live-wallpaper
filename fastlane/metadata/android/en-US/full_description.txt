Pick any video or animated GIF from your device and set it as a looping live wallpaper.

Features:
* Pick a video or GIF using the system file picker. The app keeps its own copy, so the wallpaper keeps working if the original is moved, deleted or stored in the cloud
* A preview shaped like your screen that shows exactly what the wallpaper will look like
* Adjustable playback speed (0.25x to 2.0x)
* "Fill" (crop to cover the screen) or "Fit" (show the whole frame, with letterboxing if needed)
* Drag the preview to choose which part of the picture is visible in Fill mode
* Loop just a section of a video
* Optional: move with your home screen pages (parallax), pause in Battery Saver, start from the beginning each time the wallpaper appears
* Material You colours follow the wallpaper
* Seamless looping; portrait phone videos display the right way up
* Clear messages if a file can't be played, instead of a blank wallpaper
* English and Czech

GIF notes: transparent areas appear black, and GIF files over 50 MB are not supported.

Some devices apply a live wallpaper to the home screen and lock screen together; others (such as Pixels) let you choose in the system picker.

Privacy: this app contains no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It requests no permissions at all: no internet access and no storage access. It stores a private copy of the file you choose and your settings, locally on your device.
